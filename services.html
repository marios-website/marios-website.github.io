How I Help Teams Perform Better

Training • Coaching • Enablement

Sales Fundamentals & Core Skills Training

I help new and developing salespeople build confidence and capability quickly. Sessions cover prospecting, communication, questioning, objection handling, value messaging, and closing — all delivered through real‑world scenarios and practical exercises.

Advanced Sales Training for Experienced Professionals

For senior sellers, I deliver deeper, more strategic modules including account planning, stakeholder management, negotiation, commercial thinking, and complex deal navigation. These sessions sharpen thinking, improve structure, and elevate performance.

Sales Leadership & Manager Coaching

I work with frontline managers to help them coach more effectively, run better 1:1s, drive accountability, and build high‑performing teams. This is ideal for new managers or teams needing stronger leadership rhythm and clarity.

Sales Enablement & Behaviour Change Programmes

I support organisations with messaging frameworks, customer journey mapping, CRM adoption, and embedding consistent sales behaviours. This is where training meets strategy — ensuring teams not only learn, but change how they work.

Short‑Term Contract Training Support

Perfect for organisations needing immediate capability uplift. I step in quickly, diagnose what’s happening, and deliver targeted training that stabilises performance, builds momentum, and gets teams moving in the right direction.
