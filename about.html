About Me

Sales Training • Enablement • Performance

I’ve spent most of my working life helping people get better at selling — not by giving them theory, but by giving them clarity, confidence, and practical tools they can use the very next morning. I’m naturally direct, structured, energetic, and happiest when I’m helping people move forward. I love stepping into a team, figuring out what’s really going on beneath the surface, and creating training that actually changes behaviour — not just fills a room.

Over the years I’ve worked with everyone from apprentices taking their first steps in sales to senior professionals managing complex accounts. At Pareto, I delivered high‑impact programmes across communication, negotiation, leadership behaviours, and advanced selling skills. Before that, I led teams at Checkatrade, BT Group, Graydon, and Apple — roles that taught me how salespeople think, what motivates them, and what gets in their way. My approach is simple: Make it real. Make it useful. Make it stick. I build training around real conversations, real objections, and real customer scenarios. I want people to walk out of a session thinking, “I can use that today.” I’m also big on creating an environment where people feel safe to try things, get things wrong, and grow. Sales can be tough, and confidence is half the battle — so I focus on building capability and belief. These days, I work with organisations on a short‑term or contract basis when they need someone who can hit the ground running. Whether it’s stabilising a team, upskilling managers, or injecting fresh energy into a sales function, I bring structure, pace, and a lot of practical experience. If you’re looking for a trainer who blends commercial sharpness with a down‑to‑earth coaching style, that’s where I do my best work.
